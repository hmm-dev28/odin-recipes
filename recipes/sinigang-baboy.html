Sinigang na Baboy

Description

Sinigang na Baboy is a traditional Filipino sour soup known for its
deliciously tangy and savory broth. Made with tender pork, fresh
vegetables, tomatoes, radish, eggplant, and kangkong, this comforting
dish gets its signature sour flavor from tamarind. Best served hot
with steamed rice!

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork belly or pork ribs
  • 1 packet sinigang sa sampalok mix
  • 8 cups water
  • 2 medium tomatoes (quartered)
  • 1 medium onion (quartered)
  • 1 medium radish (sliced)
  • 1 medium eggplant (sliced)
  • 1 bunch kangkong (water spinach)
  • 8–10 pieces string beans (cut into pieces)
  • 2 pieces green chili peppers
  • 1 tablespoon fish sauce (patis)
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Cooking Instructions

  1. Boil the pork
    Combine chicken, soy sauce, and half of the
    crushed garlic. Marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Add aromatics
    Add tomatoes and onion. Cover and simmer
    for 45–60 minutes or until the pork becomes tender.
  3. Add the sour flavor
    Add the sinigang mix and fish sauce. Stir well
    and adjust the sourness according to your taste.
  4. Add the vegetables
    Add radish, eggplant, string beans, and green chili peppers.
    Simmer for 5–8 minutes until the vegetables are tender but not mushy.
  5. Add kangkong and serve
    Add kangkong and cook for another 1–2 minutes. Season
    with salt and pepper as needed. Turn off the heat and serve hot.
